DESIGN THINKING CASE STUDY

Foldable Weather-Resistant Food Stall

A human-centered design project focused on helping small fast-food vendors continue operating during bad weather and load shedding.

Explore Project View Solution
01

Project Overview

Small fast-food vendors can lose significant income when bad weather prevents them from operating.

Customers may have nowhere comfortable to sit, food and equipment can become exposed to rain, and vendors may be forced to close for the day.

The Challenge

How can we help small fast-food vendors continue operating and serving customers during bad weather?

02

The Design Thinking Process

01

Empathize

Conducted interviews, observations, questionnaires and on-site research to understand vendors' experiences.

  • Vendor interviews
  • Observation
  • Questionnaires
  • Pain-point identification
02

Define

The research was transformed into a clear problem statement and How Might We question.

"How might we help small fast-food vendors continue operating and serving customers during bad weather?"
03

Ideate

Multiple possible solutions were considered before selecting the most practical concept.

  • Permanent shelter ❌
  • Food truck ❌
  • Outdoor umbrellas ❌
  • Customer tent ❌
  • Weather-resistant stall ✅
04

Prototype

Developed low-fidelity and mid-fidelity prototypes to communicate the proposed customer and vendor experience.

  • Storyboard
  • Wireframes
  • Customer journey
  • Stall setup process
05

Test

Feedback was used to improve mobility, equipment protection and energy independence.

  • Power during load shedding
  • Improved mobility
  • Better equipment protection
03

Understanding the Problem

"I need something quick to set up that protects both my food and my customers."

Prototype Development

Low-Fidelity Prototype

A storyboard demonstrating the vendor receiving a weather alert, unfolding the stall and continuing to serve customers.

Mid-Fidelity Prototype

More detailed wireframes showing the weather alert system, setup process, seating area and customer journey.

05

Final Solution

Foldable Weather-Resistant Food Stall

A portable and practical stall designed to help informal food vendors continue operating while protecting their food, equipment and customers.

01

Foldable Design

Easy to transport, set up and store.

02

Waterproof Roof

Protects food, equipment and customers.

03

Protected Seating

Creates a more comfortable customer experience.

04

Backup Battery

Supports operation during load shedding.

05

06

Electronic Payments
