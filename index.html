Design Thinking Project

Weather-Resistant Fast-Food Stall

Creative Minds Team

About the Project

This Design Thinking project focuses on helping small fast-food vendors continue operating and serving customers during bad weather.

The Problem

Small fast food vendors face serious challenges during bad weather:

  • Rain reduces customer traffic
  • Customers have nowhere comfortable to sit
  • Vendors struggle to continue operating
  • Bad weather leads to loss of income

Research

Research Methods used:

  • Interviews with fast food business owners
  • Questionnaires for customers
  • Observations of business operations

Key Feedback from Vendors

“When it rains, customers disappear and I lose almost all my sales for the day. I can’t afford to close.”
– Jabulile Mgwenya
“I need something quick to set up that protects both my food and my customers.”
– Lorraine Maphenga
“It helps us as vendors to have a shelter so that the products will be kept fresh and there is no loss during rainy days.”
– Hopewell Mnisi

Key Insights

  • Vendors need reliable protection so they can keep operating during rain.
  • Customers want a comfortable place to sit and eat even in bad weather.
  • Daily income is heavily affected by weather conditions.

How Might We Statement

How might we help small fast food vendors continue operating and serving customers during bad weather?

Proposed Solution

A foldable, weather-resistant fast-food stall designed to protect vendors, food, equipment and customers from rain and harsh weather.

Main Features

  • Foldable design (easy to set up and pack away)
  • Waterproof roof
  • Weather-resistant transparent side panels
  • Protected customer seating area
  • Built-in energy converter + backup battery (works during load shedding)
  • Supports electronic payments
  • Wheels for easy transport

Prototypes

Low-Fidelity Prototype

A hand-drawn storyboard showing the process:

  1. Vendor receives a weather alert
  2. Stall is unfolded
  3. Protective canopy and side panels are installed
  4. Customers can sit and eat comfortably
  5. Vendor continues selling during rain

Mid-Fidelity Prototype

A more detailed design showing the full customer journey and stall features, including weather alerts and protected seating.

Testing & Feedback

Positive feedback:

  • Easy to understand
  • Useful during rain

Areas for improvement:

  • Setup may take time
  • Need power during load shedding
  • Improve mobility and storage

Changes made after feedback:

  • Added energy converter
  • Added backup battery system
  • Improved portability

Expected Impact

Benefits for Business Owners

  • Continue operating during bad weather
  • Protect food and equipment
  • Increase business sustainability and income

Benefits for Customers

  • Comfortable eating environment
  • Better overall experience
  • More reliable access to food
