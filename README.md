# ☔ Foldable Weather-Resistant Food Stall  
**Design Thinking Case Study**

A complete Design Thinking project focused on helping small fast-food vendors continue operating and serving customers during bad weather.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

Small fast-food vendors (especially street and informal traders) lose significant daily income when it rains. Customers have nowhere comfortable to sit, food and equipment get wet, and many vendors are forced to close.

This project followed the full Design Thinking process to deeply understand the problem and develop a practical, low-cost solution: a **foldable, weather-resistant food stall** with protected seating and energy independence.

---

## 🔍 The Problem

Small fast food vendors face major challenges during bad weather:

- Rain sharply reduces customer traffic  
- Customers have nowhere comfortable to sit  
- Vendors struggle to continue operating  
- Bad weather leads to direct loss of income  

> “When it rains, customers disappear and I lose almost all my sales for the day. I can’t afford to close.”  
> — **Jabulile**

> “I need something quick to set up that protects both my food and my customers. This would help me stay open.”  
> — **Lorraine Maphanga**

---

## 🧭 Design Thinking Process

### 1. Empathize
- Conducted interviews and observations with real vendors  
- Used questionnaires and on-site research  
- Identified key pain points around weather, customer comfort, and daily income pressure

### 2. Define
**How Might We Statement:**  
> How might we help small fast food vendors continue operating and serving customers during bad weather?

### 3. Ideate
Ideas investigated:
- Permanent shelter ❌  
- Food truck ❌  
- Outdoor umbrellas ❌  
- Customer tent ❌  
- **Weather-Resistant Stall** ✅ (Selected)

### 4. Prototype
#### Low-Fidelity Prototype
Hand-drawn storyboard showing:
1. Vendor receives weather alert  
2. Stall is unfolded  
3. Protective canopy is installed  
4. Customers eat comfortably  
5. Vendor continues selling during rain

#### Mid-Fidelity Prototype
More detailed wireframes including:
- Weather alert system  
- Step-by-step stall setup  
- Protected seating area  
- Full customer journey

#### Enhanced Prototype Features
- Foldable design  
- Waterproof roof  
- Transparent weather-resistant side panels  
- Protected customer seating  
- Built-in energy converter  
- Backup battery system  
- Wheels for easy transport  
- Supports electronic payments

### 5. Test
**Testing Goals:**
- Is the setup process easy to understand?  
- Would vendors actually use this stall?  
- Is the seating area comfortable?  
- What changes would improve the design?

**Key Feedback Received:**
- “Need power during load shedding”  
- “Improve mobility”  
- “Better protection for equipment”

**Changes Made:**
- Added energy converter  
- Added backup battery  
- Improved portability

---

## 💡 Final Solution

**Foldable Weather-Resistant Food Stall**

A portable, professional solution that allows vendors to:
- Continue operating during rain  
- Protect food and equipment  
- Offer customers a comfortable covered seating area  
- Stay open even during load shedding (thanks to backup power)

**Key Benefits**

| For Vendors                      | For Customers                     |
|----------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Continue operating in bad weather | Comfortable eating environment    |
| Protect food & equipment         | Better overall experience         |
| Increase business sustainability | More reliable access to food      |
| Easy setup & storage             | Protected from wind and rain      |

## 📈 Expected Impact

- The solution is affordable, portable, and practical for informal traders
